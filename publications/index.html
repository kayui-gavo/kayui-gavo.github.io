Emotion Transition-Controllable Video Summarization Based on Multimodal Emotional FeaturesAPSIPA ASC 2026 · Accepted

Kewei Liu, Kazuyoshi Yoshii.

This work introduces a video-summarization setting in which a user specifies a continuous valence–arousal target path and a summary ratio. Visual, audio, and text emotion cues are fused with path-conditioned reliability-aware directed cross-attention and routing, and a dynamic-programming selector balances path adherence with content importance.

Evaluation uses TVSum with 5-fold cross-validation. In the module-level comparison, CCCmean for internal VA-path coherence increased from 0.427 ± 0.038 with confidence-based fusion to 0.531 ± 0.025 with the attention-and-router fuser.
